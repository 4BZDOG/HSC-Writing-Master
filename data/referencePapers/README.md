# Reference papers

Real NSW HSC material kept as a **calibration set** for writing and checking
questions, marking guides and exemplars. It is not shipped to students: nothing
here is imported by the app or placed under `public/`.

| File                                                                                | What it holds                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Why it is here                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `software-engineering-2025-marking-guidelines.json`                                 | NESA's 2025 marking guidelines: criteria, sample answers, content and outcome codes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | NESA does not publish the online paper; the parts its "sample full mark responses" print are in the seed as past questions (`prompt-se-hsc-2025-…`), the rest are reference only                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `enterprise-computing-2025-marking-guidelines.json`                                 | The same for Enterprise Computing                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | As above                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `geography-2024-paper-and-guidelines.json`, `geography-2025-…`                      | Every written-response part of the 2024 and 2025 papers with its guideline, including the parts that need the Stimulus Booklet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | The self-contained parts are in the seed as past questions; the rest stay here                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `english-standard-2019-2023-past-papers.json`                                       | Every written-response part of the 2019–2023 papers (Paper 1 Sections I–II, Paper 2 Sections I–III) with its guideline and NESA's Section I sample answers; Section I stimulus texts are not reproduced                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Paper 1 Section I needs the Stimulus Booklet, so it is calibration only; the rest is in the seed as past questions (`prompt-en-158`–`231`; the parts set once per text with the same wording are combined, see `docs/seed-data-playbook.md` §3)                                                                                                         |
| `legal-studies-2020-2025-past-papers.json`                                          | Every written-response part of the 2020–2025 papers (Section II Parts A and B, Section III 25-mark Options) with its guideline                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | These are the **2011** syllabus. The seed follows the new 2025 syllabus, so they are calibration for it, not shippable questions                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `<course>-2025-paper-and-guidelines.json` (12 courses)                              | Every written-response part of the 2025 paper with its guideline and NESA's sample answers, for Economics, Modern History, Ancient History, Chemistry, Physics, Earth and Environmental Science, Community and Family Studies, Society and Culture, English Studies, English EAL/D, English Extension 1 and History Extension; `skipped` lists the parts answered by drawing or needing an untranscribable figure | The source of those courses' `prompt-<course>-hsc-2025-…` questions, built with `build_past.py`. Third-party copyright texts printed as stimuli (English Section I texts, the Extension 1 and History Extension extracts) are replaced by a citation and a link to NESA's paper; where a ladder row had to open with a verb, `edits` records the change |
| `community-and-family-studies-2022-paper-and-guidelines.json`, `-2023-…`, `-2024-…` | Every written-response part of the 2022, 2023 and 2024 Community and Family Studies papers with its guideline and NESA's sample answers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | The source of `prompt-cafs-hsc-2022-…` to `prompt-cafs-hsc-2024-…`, built with `build_past.py`; `edits` records the one ladder row reworded to open with a verb                                                                                                                                                                                         |

Each file was copied from NESA's published PDFs and checked against the PDF text.
Strings are verbatim; table and code answers that do not survive as text are marked
`[not reproducible as text]`. See `docs/hsc-exam-profiles.md` for what the set shows.
