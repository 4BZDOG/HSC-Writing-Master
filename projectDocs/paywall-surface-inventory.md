# Paywall surface inventory

Status: written on branch `claude/paywall-ux`, from the source as it stands after that branch's commits. Rows marked **(changed)** were altered by this branch; the rest read as they did on `origin/main`. File and line references are to this branch and will drift; the file names are the stable part.

This is a factual map, not a plan. It exists so the next wave of paywall work can start from what each person actually sees, where the same fact is said in more than one place, and which locks are about the plan and which are about the role.

## 1. Vocabulary the app uses

| Term                | Where it comes from                                            | Value                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Plan name, full     | `PLAN_LABELS` in `services/entitlements.ts`                    | `Free`, `Band 6 Plus`, `School`                                                                                         |
| Plan name, chip     | `lockLabelFor()` in `components/UpgradeModal.tsx`              | `Plus` or `School` (short, for chips only)                                                                              |
| Monthly price       | `PLAN_PRICING.monthly`                                         | `A$7.99`, from `VITE_PLUS_MONTHLY_PRICE_DISPLAY`                                                                        |
| Yearly price        | `PLAN_PRICING.yearly`                                          | `A$59`, from `VITE_PLUS_YEARLY_PRICE_DISPLAY`                                                                           |
| Yearly saving line  | `PLAN_PRICING.yearlyNote`                                      | `Save 38% — under A$5/month`                                                                                            |
| School seat         | `PLAN_PRICING.schoolSeat`                                      | `A$4` per student per year                                                                                              |
| Free marked answers | `FREE_TIER_EVAL_LIMIT` (5), enforced by `consume_evaluation()` | 5 a day. The day boundary is UTC midnight, stated to the reader in their own clock as 10am AEST (`utils/dailyReset.ts`) |
| Daily AI calls      | `FREE_DAILY_AI_CALLS` (60), `PAID_DAILY_AI_CALLS` (300)        | Display only; the server's `ai_quota_limits` is the real budget                                                         |

Prices are presentation strings typed into environment variables. Nothing reads them from Stripe. A deployment can therefore show a price that differs from the one Stripe charges (see section 6).

## 2. What is locked, and by what

A lock is caused by the **plan** (what the account has bought or been given), by the **role** (what the account is allowed to do whatever it pays), or by both.

| Feature key                                     | Gate                                                   | Free student                                                | Plus student (own purchase)                          | Teacher, no licence          | Teacher, licensed school                                 | Admin                              |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Marked answers per day                          | Plan (metered for free only)                           | 5 a day, counter visible                                    | No limit                                             | Plus by staff perk, no limit | School, no limit                                         | School by role, no limit           |
| Question tiers 4–6 (`advancedQuestions`)        | Plan                                                   | Locked: chip, locked question panel                         | Open                                                 | Open (perk)                  | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| Sample answers, bands above 3 (`sampleAnswers`) | Plan, or withheld by server                            | Locked exemplar card                                        | Open                                                 | Open                         | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| Full marking feedback (`fullFeedback`)          | Plan                                                   | Summary and band only                                       | Full                                                 | Full                         | Full                                                     | Full                               |
| AI answer upgrades (`answerUpgrades`)           | Plan                                                   | Locked: chip and "See what Plus unlocks"                    | Open                                                 | Open                         | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| PDF report export (`pdfExport`)                 | Plan                                                   | Locked: button opens prompt, chip                           | Open                                                 | Open                         | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| Exam simulation (`examMode`)                    | Plan                                                   | Locked: press opens prompt, chip (hidden below `sm`)        | Open                                                 | Open                         | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| Outcome briefings (`outcomeBriefing`)           | Plan                                                   | Locked: chip, overlay                                       | Open                                                 | Open                         | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| AI Content Studio (`aiContentStudio`)           | Plan **and role**                                      | Controls not rendered (`canGenerate` false). No chip shown. | Plan open, still not rendered: students never author | Open                         | Open                                                     | Open                               |
| Creating courses and topics                     | Role (`canCreateCurriculum`, admin only)               | Not offered                                                 | Not offered                                          | Not offered                  | Not offered                                              | Offered                            |
| School licence purchase                         | Role (teacher or admin) plus a configured school price | Enquiry link only                                           | Enquiry link only                                    | Seat picker, "Buy N seats"   | Seat picker still shown (no check that a licence exists) | Seat picker shown (role allows it) |

Three things follow from the table.

- **Paying does not give a student the studio.** The studio is the one feature where the role is the real gate. It is hidden for students rather than locked, so no student sees a studio chip whether they pay or not. The comparison row note and the profile card say so in words (`utils/planComparison.ts`, `components/UserProfileModal.tsx` PlanCard).
- **A teacher holds Plus with no purchase** (`services/entitlements.ts` `getUserPlan`, step 3). Every plan-level lock is therefore open to them. Their only paywall contact is the School seat picker and the plan comparison.
- **Nothing ships at School.** Every `PREMIUM_FEATURES` key is priced at Plus, so `lockLabelFor()` always returns `Plus` today. The School wording exists for a deployment that uses `PLAN_FEATURE_OVERRIDES`.

When `VITE_MONETISATION_ENABLED=false`, `isFeatureLocked()` returns false for every feature, the prompt refuses to open, the counter draws nothing, and the plan comparison shows the free evaluations cell as `Unlimited`.

When monetisation is on but no Plus price is configured, the locks still show and the prompt opens, but it states that Plus is not on sale and offers no checkout button (**changed**).

## 5. Gaps the next wave should decide on (not fixed here)

1. **A teacher or admin who already holds a school licence is still offered `Buy N seats`** (row 38). Nothing in `SchoolLicencePanel.tsx` reads whether the school is licensed, and `user.stripePlan === 'school'` does not distinguish a school member from someone who bought seats. Needs a product decision on seat top-ups before code.
2. **Displayed prices are not read from Stripe.** `PLAN_PRICING` is typed into `VITE_PLUS_*_PRICE_DISPLAY`. If the display string and the Stripe Price differ, the prompt shows one amount and the charge is another. Verify against the Stripe dashboard at each price change.
3. **Whether `A$59` and `A$7.99` include GST** is not stated anywhere in the UI, and nothing in this branch checks Stripe's automatic-tax setting. Needs a decision and, if tax is added, a line in the charge copy.
4. **The teacher's checkout return for seats** (row 46): the poll looks for `stripePlan` on the signed-in profile for up to about 30 seconds. School seats are applied at sign-in (`applySchoolPlan`), so a teacher may see `Payment received. Your plan will unlock within a minute` and not unlock until they sign in again. Not verified in a browser; flagged for testing with a real school checkout.
5. **The school checkout's seat count is a quantity sent to Stripe with the same price ID for every buyer.** The UI shows `Buy N seats` but no total. Showing a total would mean computing a price from a display string, which can disagree with Stripe (item 2), so it is left out.
6. **`PlusLockChip` on phones**: the exam-mode chip is hidden below `sm` (row 7). The only signal left is the amber state.
7. **The header has no upgrade route** (row 51). The first upgrade a student sees is always inside a lock or the counter.
8. **The studio's lock states are dormant under the shipped policy.** Students never see the studio controls (`canGenerate` is false), and teachers and admins hold Plus or School, so `studioLocked` is false for every account that can see the controls. The chips and tooltips in rows 4, 5, 8, 12, 13 and 16 render only if a deployment moves the studio above the plan its staff hold. They are kept so that move cannot leave a silent gap.
