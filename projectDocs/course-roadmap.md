# Course roadmap: faculties and courses to build next

Prepared 15 November 2026. Scope: NSW Stage 6 (Years 11–12) courses, ranked for build order in the HSC Writing Master app.

This is a research document only. No course data was changed and nothing was committed.

## 1. Summary and method

- **Built today:** 21 courses, as listed in the earlier draft. The manifest was not re-read in this pass. Enterprise Computing and Software Engineering are TAS. Society and Culture and Community and Family Studies are the other shipped courses outside the core list.
- **Retired and removed:** Information Processes and Technology (IPT) and Software Design and Development (SDD) are replaced by Enterprise Computing and Software Engineering, both already built. Both are removed from the candidate list (section 6).
- **Participation measure (used throughout):** HSC course **completions in 2025**, from the NESA Board Developed course data table. Every figure in this document uses this one measure. Candidature and enrolment figures from the earlier draft are not mixed in.
- **Exam measures (used throughout):** the share of the *external HSC exam* (its marks) that is written, and the share of written marks that are *extended response* (ER). NESA pages do not state how the HSC exam is weighted against school-based marks in the final course mark. Where that is so, the document says "not stated".
- **Ranking method (section 5):**
  1. Start from 2025 completions.
  2. Multiply by a **written factor (W)** from the external exam: 1.0 for 100% written with 30% or more ER; 0.8 for 100% written with less than 30% ER (short-answer papers); 0.6 for 40–60% written; 0.4 for under 40% written or no written exam.
  3. Multiply by a **syllabus factor (S)**: 0.9 for "build now" (current syllabus, no replacement due before 2028); 0.5 for "wait" (a published replacement starts Year 11 in 2027 or earlier).
  4. Sort by the result. The factors are ordinal judgements chosen for this ranking, not statistics. Changing them changes the order, so they are stated here to be argued with.
- **Source status:** exam formats were read from NESA assessment pages through a page summariser. Completions were read from the NESA 2025 table the same way. Spot-check the figures against the page before quoting them outside the team. Syllabus dates come from a third-party guide (AusGrader, updated 4 November 2026) where NESA pages give no dates. Those are marked "third-party".

### Why the order changed from the earlier draft

- **Engineering Studies** moves into the top 7 on 2,559 completions and a 100% written exam. The draft had it unverified.
- **Investigating Science** moves up on 3,293 completions (2025) and a 100% written exam. The draft's 4,267 figure was a different measure (2024 candidature) and is dropped.
- **Drama and Music 1** stay in the top 10 on participation, but their new syllabuses are published and start in Year 11 in 2027. They become "wait" items. They are built against the 2025 syllabus, not the 2009 one.
- **Aboriginal Studies** drops from the top 10 to reserve. Its exam is 100% written and 45% ER, but its completions (827) are below every top-10 course. If the team values writing fit over volume, it can replace Music 1 at rank 10. That is a deliberate choice, not a bug.
- **Dance** drops to the reserve. Its written share depends on which Major Study option is taken, and its syllabus starts in 2027.

### Build next: top 10

**Wave 1 (build now, first):** 1 Studies of Religion I and II; 2 Visual Arts; 3 Food Technology; 5 Investigating Science.
- Studies of Religion and Food Technology have the strongest written share with the most extended response.
- Visual Arts has the largest participation of any candidate, but its written share is 50%. Questions must target the written Art Criticism and Art History paper only.
- Investigating Science is 100% written but has no named extended section. Its questions should be short-answer.

**Wave 2 (build now, after Wave 1):** 4 Industrial Technology; 6 Design and Technology; 7 Engineering Studies; 8 Agriculture.
- Industrial Technology and Design and Technology each have a 60-mark externally marked project alongside a 40-mark written paper. Only the written 40 marks are in scope for the app.
- Engineering Studies is 100% written with no extended response. Its questions are short-answer and fit the app's marking style, but the writing content is thinner.
- Agriculture has a 2028 replacement. Build it only if the team accepts roughly two years of shelf life.

**Wave 3 (wait; build against the 2025 syllabus from Year 11 2027):** 9 Drama; 10 Music 1.
- Do not start content authoring for these until the 2025 syllabus's Year 11 teaching is confirmed in 2027. Prepare the structure only.

**Reserve:** Textiles and Design (syllabus in development); Aboriginal Studies (syllabus status to verify); Dance and Music 2 and Music Extension (wait, and exam formats not verified); Science Extension (after Physics and Chemistry).

## 6. Excluded or removed

- **Information Processes and Technology and Software Design and Development: removed.** Enterprise Computing 11–12 (2022) replaces IPT, and Software Engineering 11–12 (2022) replaces SDD. Both new courses start Year 11 in 2024 and have their first HSC in 2025 (S19; S21 search result; SDD to Software Engineering from Wikipedia, not NESA). The last HSC of the old courses is inferred to be 2024 and is **not verified**. Both new courses are already built.
- **Hospitality: moved to VET.** The 8,496 figure is for the VET Industry Curriculum Framework, not a Board Developed course. It is not a candidate.
- **Mathematics: excluded.** The reason is the external exam's emphasis on calculation and symbolic working, with little extended response. This reason is not sourced to NESA in this pass. The syllabus timing is verified: Mathematics 11–12 (2024), first HSC 2027 (S19).
- **Languages: low.** Japanese Continuers (794), French Continuers (520) and Chinese Continuers (334) had 2025 completions (S1). Their written papers are in the target language, which the app cannot mark as built. Latin and Ancient Greek were not checked.
- **English Extension 2: not recommended.** The external component is the Major Work, not a written exam (section 3). Build only after the Extension 1 workflow is proven.
- **VET Industry Curriculum Frameworks and Board Endorsed courses: not recommended.** Competency-based, with little or no written exam.

## 7. Process

Use the repository skill for building a course: `.claude/skills/build-course-data/SKILL.md`. In short:

1. Confirm which syllabus is examined, using the course page on nsw.gov.au and the "Syllabus timing" note in `projectDocs/changeLog.md`.
2. Fetch NESA's data with `tools/fetch_nesa.py`. Do not rely on summarised fetches.
3. Build the structure with `tools/build_structure.py` and a config from `.claude/skills/build-course-data/configs/`, or `tools/build_course.py` if the course already exists.
4. Author questions with agents (`tools/make_jobs.py`, then the author brief), and review them.
5. Ship with the changelog entry and manifest update.

For past-paper questions, follow `docs/seed-data-playbook.md`: section 2 (how a past paper gets in), section 3 (conventions), section 4 (review) and section 5 (extraction mistakes).
